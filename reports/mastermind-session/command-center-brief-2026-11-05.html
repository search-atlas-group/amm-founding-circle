Command Center

A read-first view of fulfillment, revenue, and meeting context—so the commander can see what is happening without opening five systems.

Design brief · 2026-11-05 · Requested in the mastermind session (segments 564–587)

Not a claim of live integration.
This document defines the first version, its fields, and its review gates. Connector access and owner names must be verified before implementation.

Three contexts, one view

FulfillmentActive services, owners, stage, latest evidence, and outcome signal.
RevenueLast/next billing, amount, payment channel, collection state, and exceptions.
MeetingLast/next meeting, transcript, disposition, sentiment, and missed-meeting signal.
Commander viewOne source of truth with timestamped evidence and visible unknowns.

First principle

Unknown is a valid state. A blank connector or missing evidence is not a healthy status.

Acceptance gate

Enumerate active clients, show their active services, give each service one owner, and link to evidence. Do not infer health from missing data.

Implementation sequence

  1. Prove a real read-only Zoom fetch and source identity.
  2. Store private meeting metadata and transcripts with revision keys.
  3. Add disposition and next-meeting checks.
  4. Add sentiment only after the first layers are reliable.
  5. Review the first view with the named owners before any external write.
