# Candidate intake template

Copy this block into `backlog.md` for every new candidate, then complete every field before marking it `Ready`.

```markdown
### [Title]
- Status: Idea | Draft | Ready | Shared
- Source: [session, transcript, repo path, member request, or research URL]
- Member value: [specific problem this helps a member solve]
- Target session: Mon | Tue | Thu, [date or `next available`]
- Owner: [person or PM]
- Evidence/demo path: [repo-relative file, URL, or reproducible command]
- Setup needed: [accounts, sample data, screen share, or `none`]
- Next action: [one concrete action and owner]
- After sharing: [permanent destination, follow-up, or `no follow-up`]
```

## Intake gate

- A source is named and accessible, or explicitly marked pending.
- The member value is specific enough to explain in one sentence.
- The target session and owner are assigned.
- The evidence/demo path exists before `Ready` is assigned.
- A candidate is not marked `Shared` until the session packet or session notes show it was actually used.
