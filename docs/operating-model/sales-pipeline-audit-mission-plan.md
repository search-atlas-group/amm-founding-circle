# Sales pipeline audit — mission plan

**Origin:** mastermind session, segments 596–620  
**Status:** draft for review; analysis first, no CRM writes or outreach  
**Outcome:** identify the sales-pipeline stage with the greatest revenue leverage and produce a sequenced repair plan.

## Four-stage map

### Stage 1 — Traffic → CRM lead

Map how a visitor arrives, lands, submits or calls, becomes a CRM lead, and receives attribution. Measure volume, conversion, source, form friction, and time to first response.

### Stage 2 — Pre-meeting

Map the experience after lead capture and before the meeting. Measure booking rate, confirmation, show rate, no-show recovery, pre-call education, and lead qualification.

### Stage 3 — Meeting experience

Audit the meeting structure, discovery, offer, proof, guarantee, objection handling, close, and next step. Identify the prospect’s experience and where energy or trust is lost.

### Stage 4 — Post-meeting follow-up

Map the path from meeting outcome to proposal, follow-up, decision, close, onboarding, or loss. Measure speed, number and quality of touches, owner, next action, and loss reasons.

## Inputs to inventory

- Website analytics and conversion events.
- CRM / GHL lead and opportunity stages.
- Calendar and meeting records.
- Forms and qualification fields.
- Meeting transcripts or notes, when permissioned.
- Proposals, offer pages, guarantees, and sales collateral.
- Closed-won / closed-lost outcomes.

The session named CRM, Zoom, GA4, and other connected systems as candidate sources; access and scope must be verified before use (segments 611–613).

## Processing sequence

1. Inventory each source and its freshness.
2. Draw the actual customer journey, not the intended one.
3. Quantify each stage where data exists; label gaps as `UNKNOWN`.
4. Produce a stage-by-stage finding list with evidence.
5. Score impact, confidence, effort, and dependency.
6. Rank the four stages by expected revenue leverage.
7. Compare the data-driven ranking with the operator’s judgment.
8. Draft a lane plan for the highest-leverage stage.

## Definition of done

- [ ] All four stages have an evidence-backed map.
- [ ] The weakest / highest-leverage stage is identified or explicitly marked unknown.
- [ ] Every recommendation names the evidence and owner needed.
- [ ] A short mission brief exists for the first repair lane.
- [ ] No external copy, offer, CRM record, or outreach is changed by the audit.

## Review gate

The commander reviews the analysis, chooses the first stage, and approves the repair lane. A separate mission is required for any CRM write, website change, offer change, or outreach.

## Out of scope

- Sending prospect messages.
- Editing the CRM or website.
- Changing pricing or guarantees.
- Declaring a stage healthy from missing data.
- Building a “tombstone” report that only lists activity without decisions.
