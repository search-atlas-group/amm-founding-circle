# Find the handoff

Instructions for a member's own agent. Read this file and do the task. Do not assume any folder layout, repository, or platform. The member's setup is the setup.

## The task

Look at the member's last two weeks of real work. Find the one thing they keep doing by hand. Propose the agent that would take it. Then stop. Do not build anything.

## How

1. Ask which sources you may read, if the member has not named them. Sent mail, closed tasks, files they edited, a client record. Read only what they approve.
2. Read the last fourteen days of those sources.
3. Count what the member personally did more than once. Ignore anything already handled by an agent or an automation.
4. Pick the single strongest candidate. Prefer the one that costs them the most time and recurs on a predictable rhythm.
5. Propose one agent for it: the job in one sentence, the trigger, the choices it would make (one option is doing nothing), what it may read, and what it must never do.
6. Say how sure you are, and name the one fact you were missing that would change the recommendation.

## Rules

- Do not build, scaffold, or turn on the agent you propose.
- Do not send email, post to chat, publish, or spend money.
- Do not change anything in a live client account.
- If a source is off limits, skip it and say so. Do not guess what was in it.

## What you return

1. The candidates you counted, with the evidence for each.
2. The one you picked, and why the others lost.
3. The proposed agent, in the six lines above.
4. Your confidence, and the one missing fact.

Leave it where the member can open it. Do not polish it after.

## What the member does with it

The member reads the finding and writes one correction: you found the right work, you found the wrong work, or the real work was somewhere you could not see. That correction is the point. Do not revise the output to match it.

Fill in `templates/one-job-agent.md`. A completed version is in `templates/one-job-agent-example.md`.
