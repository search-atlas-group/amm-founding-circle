What to do — in order

6 moves
#MoveEffect
1Fix the ratio before adding throughput
At 1.4× inflow, a wider fleet ends September with ~900 open beads, not fewer. Cap agent decomposition: no auto-created children without a closed parent.		the whole game
2Burn the 118 on codex-gw — no guard changes
These are already eligible, already scored, already launchable today. At the measured 62% success rate that is ~73 real closures.		−73 beads
3Run the bake-off as research missions
Research missions skip the codex-gw pin, so claude-gw / kimi can be compared on real work without editing a safety guard.		3-way, runs today
4Freeze tier 2 + 3 backlogs
Defer the 294 marketing-site and dashboard beads. Not deleted — they stop competing with loop work.		−294 from active
5Impose a WIP limit
Cap in_progress at ~3 per repo; reset the rest to open. A bead in progress for 30+ days is lying to you.		96 → ~30
6Auto-defer at 90 days
This is what let 23 macdaddy beads rot silently.		prevents recurrence
The order matters. Moves 2–6 are all throughput. Move 1 is the only one that changes the trajectory — every previous burn-down was undone by the next decomposition burst.
