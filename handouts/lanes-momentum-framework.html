Lanes Momentum Framework

A small board for meaningful outcomes, one accountable owner, and daily movement.

The board

3–4 active lanes
Rank left-to-right by current impact.
3-week horizon
Week 1 specific; later weeks lighter.
One owner
Support is allowed. Shared accountability is not.
Done shelf
Green work leaves the active board.

Daily lane walk

  1. Start with the highest-impact lane.
  2. Ask what changed.
  3. Move completed cards to Done.
  4. Resolve red cards: unblock, narrow, reassign, defer, or stop.
  5. Recommit the next card and its evidence.

Commander rules

  • Do the thought work before assigning execution.
  • Give the owner a short brief and definition of done.
  • Do not own every lane.
  • Use agents as autonomous executors, not chat-based errand runners.
  • Review output against evidence, not activity.

Bad board test

If the board is a diary, task dump, or artifact wall, it cannot answer what matters, who owns it, what is blocked, or what is complete. Narrow it.
