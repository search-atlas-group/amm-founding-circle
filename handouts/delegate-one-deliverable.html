It chose.
That's the whole test.

Hand one deliverable you already sell to your own agent. Bring back what it decided.

The difference

A workflow

Follows the steps. Every input gets the same action. It never asks whether the action belongs.

An agent

Judges first. It reads the situation, chooses from options you wrote down, and can choose to do nothing.

Five checks. All five, or it isn't an agent.

  1. One named job, not a list of things it can do.
  2. Something starts it while you are away. If you have to open it and type, it is a workflow.
  3. Its boundary is written down. What it may read, and what it must never do.
  4. It chose. Faced with a real case, it picked one option, or correctly picked none, and wrote the reason.
  5. The output and the reason were waiting somewhere you could open afterward.
