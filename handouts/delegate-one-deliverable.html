Find the work you still do by hand.

Point an agent at your last two weeks. Let it find the one recurring job, and propose the agent that would take it. Build nothing.

What you do

01

Point it

At your real last two weeks. Mail you sent, tasks you closed, edits you made.

02

Let it look

It counts what repeated and names the single best candidate.

03

It proposes

One agent: the job, the decisions it would make, the boundary, and where it's unsure.

04

You correct it

Say whether it found the right work. The correction is what you bring.

What its output looks like

Weekly client report6 times
Proposal follow-up3 times
Invoice chase2 times

Its pick: the weekly client report. Only one job qualifies. The rest is noise.

What you bring

  1. Its finding, untouched. The pattern, the count, and the evidence it cited.
  2. The proposed agent. One job, the choices it would make, what it must never do.
  3. Your correction. Right work, wrong work, or work you do that it couldn't see. One paragraph.
Thursday we ask one question: which of these is worth turning on, and what has to be true first. Nothing sends, nothing publishes, nothing spends. The brief your agent reads: find-the-handoff.md
